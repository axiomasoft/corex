# P2.2 — tenancy storage adapter proof receipt

**Recorded:** 2026-09-13  
**Scope:** D4-local fake-cloud storage only; no SDK, credential, provider, backup,
restore, archive, move, or external operation was used.

## Delivered boundary

- `ContextTenantStorage` is the `TenantStorage` binding supplied by
  `TenancyServiceProvider` for initialized tenancy contexts. It constructs a
  private `ScopedFilesystem` directly and never returns a Stancl-mutated named
  disk or calls `Storage::disk()`.
- `tenancy.storage.durable_driver=local|fake-s3`; both approved modes use the
  installed local Laravel adapter. `fake-s3` is explicitly a local
  object-prefix test backend, not a real S3 compatibility claim. Ephemeral
  storage always uses its separate trusted local root.
- Handles capture their account and root. The inherited scoped guard rejects
  traversal, encoded traversal, cross-account retained handles, and handles
  after tenant context clears. Roots are `.../tenants/{account-id}` exactly
  once; no Stancl prefix is read or appended.
- The source gate permits `FilesystemManager` only in the owning adapter and
  its DI provider. A disposable raw `Storage::disk()` probe made the gate RED
  (exit 1); it was removed before final checks.
