# P4.5 — local fenced credential proof

**Result:** local GREEN under D8 only. `FencedCredentialIssuer` performs the
pair-fence commit and session, optional remember, and metadata writes inside
one outer transaction. A denied fence or database failure produces a typed
denial and rolls the complete transaction back. The response fixture clears
all queued session and remember cookies once denial is known.

The disposable PostgreSQL tests prove both-or-neither issuance, remember-off,
denial cookie stripping, and rollback after a duplicate remember write. This
does not add an auth guard, provider registration, production session schema,
actual DI/AP, or a membership release.

Validation: root Pint/PHPStan, 104 non-PG tests, 101 PG tests, and 5 arch
tests; tenancy Pint/PHPStan/full Pest; focused Feature 3/8 and PG 1/3. The
repository-wide mutation gate caught all 30 sabotage cases. No migration,
runtime registration, dependency, commit, private access, or external write.
