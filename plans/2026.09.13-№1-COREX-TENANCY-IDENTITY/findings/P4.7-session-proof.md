## Local composition

`ReferenceMembershipProjection` composes the existing internal pair store, expiry, fenced credential issuer, response cookie queue, durable inbox, and pull-only refresh coordinator. It is not container-bound, a guard, middleware, job, provider registration, or public API.

A grant requires an active snapshot for the exact account/identity pair and a local lease below the 45-second boundary. The pair store records which operation applied its authority snapshot: a regrant is refused until that current operation has applied a snapshot. Revocation first commits the durable barrier, clears queued cookies, then removes only matching local session/remember fixture rows. A delayed pre-revoke grant cannot pass the newer pair operation/revision fence.

Verified triggers remain inert inbox work until a successful pull acknowledges them. Restore keeps the pair denied and clears prepared cookies; it cannot issue a credential.
