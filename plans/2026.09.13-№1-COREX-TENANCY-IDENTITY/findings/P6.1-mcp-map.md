## Readiness rule

`PENDING` means that no registered MCP handler and no handler test were established
by the P1.1 source qualification. The exact search was:

```bash
rg -n --glob '*.php' --glob '*.json' --glob '*.yml' --glob '*.yaml' --glob '*.xml' --glob '*.neon' \
  'get_account_status|retry_provisioning|replenish_pool|list_cells|get_cell_health|start_migration_batch|get_migration_status|move_account|get_tenant_usage|start_export|get_certificates_expiry|get_identity|list_memberships|grant_membership|revoke_membership|resync_account_users|get_sync_status|invalidate_switcher_cache|list_invitations' \
  packages/core packages/tenancy packages/auth tests .github
```

It returned no matches. Thus an existing package symbol is only a related source
anchor; it is not a handler. In particular, a future `TenantStorage`,
`CentralApiClient`, `AccountSwitcher`, or claim contract from P1.2 remains a
proposal, not readiness evidence.

Every future `READY` claim needs, together, a registered handler, its exact source
and test SHA, a trusted control-plane actor, named consumer policy enforcement,
account/cell binding derived from trusted context, and the negative checks below.
No handler may accept an account, identity, policy decision, path, provider, or
acting actor as an unchecked tenant-agent value. A package must not bypass that
consumer policy. These are prerequisites, not work performed by this item.

## Readiness negatives and result

Before any row becomes `READY`, its handler proof must show the appropriate negative
case: unauthenticated or tenant-agent caller denied; wrong account/cell/product denied;
unchecked actor/policy/path/provider input denied; and no package-to-central/control-plane
authority inversion. Writes additionally need their declared confirmation gate; account
move needs both confirmations; export needs bounded provider/archival failure handling;
membership revoke must not succeed on uncertain central outcome. TLS remains
external-only unless a separately authorized external provider integration is delivered.

The result is deliberately **19 PENDING rows**. It records qualified existing symbols
where available, but does not manufacture a tool handler, registration, publication,
remote call, central mutation, or readiness claim.
