## DI-31/32 timing and capacity separation

DI-31 requires measured priority-expiry admission and DB recovery/commit behavior under boundary, over-budget, failover and backlog faults. It distinguishes physical session/credential cleanup from an authorization denial; a deny at the lease boundary alone does not prove cleanup within 60 seconds. DI-32 requires a current-month instrumented population of all committed events, including no-data, missing and late observations, timestamp uncertainty and tails. Neither a proposed FE-60 decomposition nor an unfinished measurement window may become GREEN.

The timing receipt must also prove the paused-holder cases: expiry competes with a holder paused *inside* the pair transaction, termination/rollback is observed, no late positive COMMIT or publisher escapes, and comparison-to-durable-COMMIT is bounded (or an accepted equivalent is specifically evidenced). It identifies a compatible shared clock domain or denies cross-domain comparison; raw host ticks, a second process-local clock read, or a row lock alone are insufficient.
