# P10 — effective receipt qualification design, p10-effective-receipts/1

RAG:— Design-only contract. No actual P1/P2/P3/P9 producers, deployment, AP, provider, timing, monthly window, actor acceptance or external grant is invented. Current fixture helpers and local GREEN do not prove actual adoption. General verified practices live in research/P10-design-rag.md, D4/D5/D6 mandatory neutral external retrieval. Existing Contracts and A01–A09 remain unchanged; P10 writes only local immutable sanitized evidence copies/findings and a local verifier prepared P10.1.

## Decisions and boundaries

C1 same-cohort/source/profile/environment/actor subcondition envelope, never source-only DI closure. C2 complete actual every-edge/every-entry inventories, certificate retention and TLS proxy peer trust; no default URI/AS/clock inferred. C3 independently allocated witness outside entire central+tenant rollback set, no new PKI/transparency engine. C4 FE-60 authorization deny versus physical committed cleanup and conservative actual clock/budget bounds. C5 complete closed-month census and upper-bound percentile, missing/late not filtered. C6 selected actual provider/control-plane acceptance, no S3-compatible equals IAM security assumption. Numerical45/5/10/30/300/600/profile limits are accepted local obligations from gates, not universal web-derived or hardcoded library defaults.

**Execution mode:** all nine items plan-exec, no Authority: imports/read-only qualification only. G-CENTRAL/G-CLOUD/G-TIMING/G-STORAGE/G-SCHEMA/G-API denote required source/acceptance evidence, never a grant to call endpoints, register clients, retrieve private source/credentials, run fault injection, restore/drop/move DBs, initiate paid provisioning/ACME/IAM/fleet/jobs or change foreign repos/plans. Read-only verification uses sanitized receipts already delivered by independently authorized producers; no tool sends a message. If external work is missing: concrete request packet + named owner/resources/commands/expected/rollback/required owning authority, then wait on new immutable receipt. The first preparation is an output of P10.1; repeated unchanged discovery does not grow documents or become GREEN. Source mismatch is affected owning repair/design contract choice, not an inline foreign change.

P10.1 prepares local verifier and meaningful refusal fixtures before any receipt acceptance. P10.2 central and P10.3 cloud branches independently depend P10.1; P10.4/5/6/7 depend qualified central+cloud target; P10.8 ops independently consumes intake plus selected source seals. P10.9 consumes all eight. P3 and P9 upstream outputs may retain external pending; P10 never waits for its own seal through upstream, no execution cycle. Source/test/installed/released/hosted/effective/executed/accepted stages distinct. NO-GO packets permit preparation only; P10.2–9 require actual GREEN acceptance and never close on missing receipt.
