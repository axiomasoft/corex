# P16-Q2 — Composer autoload boundaries

Status: `primary-confirmed`

The Perplexity follow-up was used to isolate MoonShine terminology. The structural decision was
then checked against Composer's official schema documentation:

- one PSR-4 namespace prefix may map to an array of directories;
- `autoload-dev` is root-only and is intended to keep test classes out of production autoload;
- strict/optimized autoload checks can expose invalid or ambiguous mappings.

Primary URLs:

- https://getcomposer.org/doc/04-schema.md#psr-4
- https://getcomposer.org/doc/04-schema.md#autoload-dev
- https://getcomposer.org/doc/03-cli.md#dump-autoload-dumpautoload

These facts support D92 and D94. CoreX directory names, item split and subsystem maps remain
repository decisions rather than externally sourced prescriptions.
