# D107 — Attributes is classified by producer-adjusted census as profile B

**Actor:** plan-designer/codex-gpt-5.6-sol/high
**Evidence:** `RAG:—` DA12 establishes a deterministic lower bound above 40 PHP files
after P10 producers, so the profile-A premise is already false before execution.

## Решение

D91's producer-adjusted rule remains, but `attributes` is explicitly a profile-B
package. P10.8 owns its physical-compatible subsystem structure. P16.11 consumes the
post-P10 census and proves a B-conformant no-move verdict against that structure; it does
not leave an unowned conditional move. The remaining 11 compact packages stay in the
Profile-A matrix. The threshold itself is unchanged.

## Почему

Known producer growth must be incorporated during design. Classification is not a
runtime exception and does not force a gratuitous move when B is already satisfied.

## Consequences

P10.8/P16.11 share one post-P10 census; P16.13 and P17.10 verify the resulting profile
manifest rather than expecting 12 A packages.
