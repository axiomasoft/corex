### P10.4 — Reversible schema evolution

**Intent:** Реализовать rename/type evolution через dual-read/write, resumable
backfill, verification, switch и explicit contract.

**Why:** `P10-design.md` §4; D52; D102. Одношаговая смена теряет concurrent writes.

**Scope Included:** transition validator; transformer registry; backfill runner;
checkpoints/digests; JSONB/EAV dual paths; abort/rollback; PG concurrency tests.

**Scope Excluded:** generic workflows; automatic drop; rewrite
`QueuedSchemaOperations`; index creation P10.5.

**Implementation Rules:** State graph is closed. Batch cursor advances after commit;
record version rechecked under lock. Transformer declared and pure. No drop before
verify+grace. Rename reserves old alias and new key to one attribute during migration;
contract removes the old reservation only after verification and grace. Both rollback points
from §4 are executable.

**Code Guidance:** Keep evolution orchestration in Schema; adapt stores in their
existing Engine paths and let P10.8 perform the physical move. Reuse current
transaction/token mechanisms.

**Validation:** Rename and type change on both engines; expand→switch→contract atomically changes current reservations while preserving all revisions; rollback restores the prior projection; kill/retry every stage;
concurrent write preserved; digest mismatch blocks switch; rollback before/after switch.

**Deliverables:** Evolution/backfill components, engine adapters, PG evidence.
