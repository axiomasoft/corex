### P11.8 — Extension contract suite, ratchet gate and final proof

**Intent:** Дать каждой typed projection переиспользуемый contract suite,
закрепить authority/projection matrix ratchet-гейтом и собрать final P11 receipt.

**Why:** `research/P11-design.md` §1, §12–§13; D56. Один compiler не
доказывает, что новая проекция не ввела второй authority или
mutable registration path.

**Scope Included:** Framework-neutral extension contract helper; duplicate/order/freeze/version
fixtures; root gate against unclassified registry-like surfaces; final validation matrix; policy,
root changelog and receipt.

**Scope Excluded:** Rewriting API/panels/search/attributes; package-specific business assertions;
new runtime registry; release/tag; P17 acceptance fixtures.

**Implementation Rules:** Helper accepts adapters/fixtures, not Laravel container globals. Suite must
assert identity uniqueness, deterministic ordering, mutation rejection/detachment, stable version
and mismatch outcome. Gate owns an explicit allow/classification list: authority, projection,
boot-built catalogue, data catalogue or driver factory. `SearchManager` is driver factory. Update
only root changelog; package changelogs are not release ledgers.

**Code Guidance:** Keep fixture format scalar and reusable by P13/P17. Gate should fail on a newly
introduced `*Registry|*Catalogue|*Manager` candidate until classified, while suppressing false
positives only through reviewed entries. Receipt records commands, fingerprints and negative cases.

**Validation:** Run contract fixtures for compiled registry, entity collector, workflow/wakeup stamps,
including unrelated-fingerprint replay and legacy identity-algorithm rows,
and both consumers; package suites for modules/workflow/wakeup/tasks-workflow/notifications; root
lint/analyse/test plus isolated PG closure; gate detects an injected unclassified fixture; changelog
and policy agree with D56–D59. Skipped PG or consumer suite is not green.

**Deliverables:** Reusable contract suite, registry-classification ratchet, complete P11 receipt,
updated extension policy and one root release note.
