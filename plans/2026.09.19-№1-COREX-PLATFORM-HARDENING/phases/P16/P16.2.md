### P16.2 — Notifications subsystem-first physical layout

**Intent:** Move notifications into Inbox, Delivery, Digest and Preferences without changing FQCN or runtime semantics.

**Why:** `research/P16-design.md` §4; notification delivery currently crosses Contracts/Data/Internal/Jobs/Commands, while a speculative Template subsystem has no owner.

**Scope Included:** Four physical subsystems; Composer sub-prefix mappings; package tests and move receipt.

**Scope Excluded:** Empty Template folder, payload/schema change, delivery retry semantics, wakeup contract changes, DDL and new channels.

**Inputs:** `research/P16-design.md`; `decisions/D91-producer-adjusted-structure-profiles.md`; `decisions/D92-physical-profile-fqcn-stable.md`; `phases/P12/P12.1.md`; `phases/P15/P15.7.md`.

**Files:** `packages/notifications/src/**`; `packages/notifications/tests/**`; `packages/notifications/composer.json`; `packages/notifications/phpstan.neon`; `plans/2026.09.19-№1-COREX-PLATFORM-HARDENING/findings/P16-move-receipts.md`.

**Required Reads:** 1) `phases/P16/P16.md`; 2) `research/P16-design.md` §0–§4; 3) `decisions/D92-physical-profile-fqcn-stable.md`; 4) `phases/P12/P12.1.md`; 5) `phases/P15/P15.7.md`; 6) `packages/notifications/composer.json`.

**Implementation Rules:** A value shared by two subsystems receives Shared only with a receipt naming both owners; otherwise follow §4. No source-level namespace edits.

**Validation:** notifications lint/analyse/test/test:pg green; strict Composer checks; identical FQCN/signature manifest; no `src/{Data,Internal}` catch-all remains; no empty Template directory.

**Deliverables:** Four-subsystem profile B and before/after Inbox/Delivery/Digest/Preferences spans.
