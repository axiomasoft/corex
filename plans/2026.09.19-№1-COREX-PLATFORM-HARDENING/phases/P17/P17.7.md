### P17.7 — Supported-version upgrade proof

**Intent:** Reproduce upgrade from each applicable package's latest supported immutable release to the exact `1.0.0` candidate in pristine consumers.

**Why:** `research/P17-design.md` §7; D96; DOD-21. A monorepo tag or source diff does not prove Composer resolution, discovery, migrations and persisted compatibility for split packages.

**Scope Included:** Read-only baseline discovery; reviewed immutable baseline manifest; populated fixtures; candidate update; idempotent retry/forward recovery; API/schema/config comparison; never-published classification.

**Scope Excluded:** Customer production data; arbitrary historical versions; rollback after committed migration; remote writes; re-tagging a baseline; product repair.
