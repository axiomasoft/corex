### P8.2 — Tenancy: Runtime, Database, Provisioning

**Intent:** Вынести register()-биндинги runtime, database и provisioning
(включая `AccountLifecycle`) в три registrar'а без смены lifetime.

**Why:** A40. `RAG:✅` P8-Q4: доказательство — bound + concrete +
lifetime. D43: AccountLifecycle ∈ Provisioning. D44: каталог `Laravel/`.

**Scope Included:** три класса §5.2–§5.3; цикл `registrars()` с этими
тремя; хелперы и тесты §9 на строках этих модулей; CHANGELOG; receipt.

**Scope Excluded:** Identity и далее (P8.3); boot-фаза (P8.4); смена
порядка bootstrapper'ов; MembershipRegistrar; `singleton` → `scoped`.

**Inputs:** `research/P8-design.md` §5.1–§5.3, §9; `findings/P8-binding-catalog.md`;
`packages/tenancy/src/Support/TenancyOptions.php`.

**Files:** `packages/tenancy/src/Laravel/RuntimeRegistrar.php`; `packages/tenancy/src/Laravel/DatabaseRegistrar.php`; `packages/tenancy/src/Laravel/ProvisioningRegistrar.php`; `packages/tenancy/src/TenancyServiceProvider.php`; `packages/tenancy/tests/Concerns/AssertsContainerBindings.php`; `packages/tenancy/tests/Feature/CompositionBindingsTest.php`; `CHANGELOG.md`

**Required Reads:** 1) `phases/P8/P8.md`; 2) `research/P8-design.md` §0, §5.1–§5.3, §9; 3) `research/P8-design-rag.md` §1 (P8-Q4); 4) `decisions/D43-modules-from-measured-bindings.md`; 5) `decisions/D44-laravel-folder-for-registrars.md`; 6) `plans/2026.09.19-№1-COREX-PLATFORM-HARDENING/findings/P8-binding-catalog.md`; 7) `packages/tenancy/src/TenancyServiceProvider.php`; 8) `packages/core/src/Support/PackageRegistrar.php`; 9) `packages/tenancy/src/Support/TenancyOptions.php`.
