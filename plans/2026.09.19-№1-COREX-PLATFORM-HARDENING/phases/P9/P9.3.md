### P9.3 — Membership без `Internal`

**Intent:** Разложить весь Membership по десяти связным подсистемам и
поглотить `Central` в `Membership/Transport`.

**Why:** `P9-design.md` §2, §5; D12/D47. `Internal` совпадает со всей
границей Membership и не несёт информации.

**Scope Included:** `Credentials`, `Expiration`, `Identity`, `Inbox`, `Persistence`,
`Projection`, `Refresh`, `Switcher`, `Timing`, `Transport`; internal namespace rewrites;
membership tests; receipt.

**Scope Excluded:** membership public contracts; new registrar; protocol semantics; models/events
других contexts; transport schema.

**Inputs:** P9.1 map/baseline; `research/P9-design.md` §5; D12/D43/D47.

**Files:** `packages/tenancy/src/Membership/**`; `packages/tenancy/src/Central/**`;
`packages/tenancy/src/Actions/ApplyMembershipProjection.php`;
`packages/tenancy/tests/Unit/Membership/**`; `packages/tenancy/tests/Feature/Membership/**`;
`packages/tenancy/composer.json`; `findings/P9-receipts.md`; `CHANGELOG.md`

**Required Reads:** 1) `phases/P9/P9.md`; 2) `research/P9-design.md` §0, §2, §5;
3) P9.1 map/baseline; 4) `decisions/D12-internal-is-not-the-package-boundary.md`;
5) `decisions/D43-modules-from-measured-bindings.md`;
6) `decisions/D47-central-and-database-have-domain-owners.md`;
7) current `Membership/**`; 8) current `Central/**`; 9) membership tests.

**Implementation Rules:** Каждый из 39 Membership-файлов и 12 Central-файлов
имеет строку в карте. Не создавать новый `Internal`. `Expiry`→`Expiration`
меняет путь/namespace, не имена классов.

**Code Guidance:** Слить два Transport-источника, но не дублировать protocol
DTO. Пустой sweep по `Membership\\Internal|src/Central` обязателен.

**Validation:** package lint/analyse/test; `composer test:pg`; map coverage; empty forbidden
sweep; membership transport/projection behavior и fingerprint green.

**Deliverables:** Membership tree, imports/tests, receipt, root CHANGELOG.
