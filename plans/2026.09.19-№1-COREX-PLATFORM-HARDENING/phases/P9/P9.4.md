### P9.4 — Account и Provisioning

**Intent:** Дать account lifecycle и physical-database provisioning явных owners,
убрав корневые `Actions`, `Database`, `Lifecycle`, `Migrations` и связанные rows.

**Why:** `P9-design.md` §2, §6; D47. Место хранения или тип слоя
не заменяют owner-возможность.

**Scope Included:** Account models/events/lifecycle; Provisioning action/database/jobs/migrations/models;
stable model/event/job FQCN maps; tests; receipt.

**Scope Excluded:** files under `packages/tenancy/database/**`; schema/table/connection changes;
relay scheduling; account FSM behavior.

**Inputs:** P9.1 map/baseline; `research/P9-design.md` §3, §6; D46/D47.

**Files:** `packages/tenancy/src/Account/**`; `packages/tenancy/src/Provisioning/**`;
`packages/tenancy/src/{Actions,Database,Lifecycle,Migrations,Models,Events,Jobs}/**`;
`packages/tenancy/composer.json`; `packages/tenancy/tests/**`;
`findings/P9-receipts.md`; `CHANGELOG.md`

**Required Reads:** 1) `phases/P9/P9.md`; 2) `research/P9-design.md` §0, §2, §3, §6;
3) P9.1 map/baseline; 4) D46; 5) D47; 6) current Account/Provisioning source rows;
7) `packages/tenancy/database/**`; 8) account/provisioning tests.

**Implementation Rules:** Database migration tree read-only. Public/serialized FQCN из §3
сохраняются. Не переносить membership/identity jobs в этом item.

**Code Guidance:** Model distribution и job ownership — таблица §2; проверять
не только class load, но table/connection и FSM output.

**Validation:** package lint/analyse/test; `composer test:pg`; account FSM/provisioning tests;
stable FQCN green; `git diff --exit-code -- packages/tenancy/database`.

**Deliverables:** Account/Provisioning tree, compatibility maps/tests, receipt, root CHANGELOG.
