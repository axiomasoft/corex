### P9.5 — Identity и Authentication/OIDC

**Intent:** Сделать OIDC протокольным adapter'ом Authentication, а identity sync —
самостоятельным context, не меняя routes и optional predicate.

**Why:** `P9-design.md` §2, §3, §7. Протокол обслуживает
authentication, но не является отдельным bounded context.

**Scope Included:** `Authentication/{Oidc,Http/Controllers,Exceptions}`; Identity models/jobs/events;
PSR-4 compatibility; route/middleware fingerprint; tests; receipt.

**Scope Excluded:** auth package; OIDC protocol behavior/config keys/routes; impersonation;
renaming classes; new provider.

**Inputs:** `findings/P9-tenancy-map.md`; `findings/P9-compatibility-baseline.json`; `research/P9-design.md` §3, §7; `packages/tenancy/src/Laravel/AuthenticationRegistrar.php`.

**Files:** `packages/tenancy/src/Authentication/**`; `packages/tenancy/src/Identity/**`;
`packages/tenancy/src/{Oidc,Http,Exceptions,Models,Events,Jobs}/**`;
`packages/tenancy/composer.json`; `packages/tenancy/tests/**`;
`findings/P9-receipts.md`; `CHANGELOG.md`

**Required Reads:** 1) `phases/P9/P9.md`; 2) `research/P9-design.md` §0, §2, §3, §7;
3) `findings/P9-tenancy-map.md` `findings/P9-compatibility-baseline.json`; 4) `decisions/D46-context-first-physical-compatible-fqcn.md`; 5) `research/P8-design.md` §2.1, §5.4;
6) `packages/tenancy/src/Oidc/OidcClient.php`; 7) `packages/tenancy/routes/oidc.php`;
8) `packages/tenancy/tests/Feature/AccountLifecycleTest.php`.

**Implementation Rules:** Route name/method/URI/action/middleware order байтово равны
baseline; `oidc.enabled=false` остаётся нулевым composition. Stable seam FQCN сохранить.

**Code Guidance:** Точная карта — §2/§7. Не создавать `Authentication/Internal`
и не переносить AuthenticationRegistrar из `Laravel/`.

**Validation:** package lint/analyse/test; `composer test:pg`; enabled/disabled OIDC tests;
route/middleware/fingerprint equality; optimized autoload green.

**Deliverables:** Authentication/Identity tree, compatibility maps/tests, receipt, root CHANGELOG.
