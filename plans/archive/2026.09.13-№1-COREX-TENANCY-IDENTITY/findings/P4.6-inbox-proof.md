# P4.6 — local durable inbox and refresh proof

**Result:** local GREEN under D8 only. Verified trigger data is admitted to a
disposable durable inbox by its stable logical digest; a redelivery updates its
delivery envelope but cannot create a conflicting event. The inbox never grants
membership. `RefreshCoordinator` claims one expiring owner lease, runs the pull
outside a transaction, and acknowledges only after that pull succeeds.

Focused tests prove expired input causes no inbox write, same logical delivery
deduplicates, failed refresh remains pending, and a lease-expired crashed owner
can be retried. This is not endpoint/queue registration, a central signing
writer, capacity/retention acceptance, real DI/AP, or a production release.

Validation: root Pint/PHPStan, 104 non-PG tests, 101 PG tests, and 5 arch
tests; tenancy Pint/PHPStan/full Pest; focused Feature 3/8 and PG 1/3. No
migration, runtime registration, dependency, commit, private access, or
external write.
