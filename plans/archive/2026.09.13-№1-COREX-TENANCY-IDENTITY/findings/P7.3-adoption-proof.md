# P7.3 — local public membership-profile adoption

Date: 2026-09-14

## Delivered local package boundary

- `CentralApiClient` now uses the strict `corex-membership/1` DTOs. A read
  requires `identityId`, `accountId`, `product`, and a fresh `requestId`; a
  revoke returns `RevokeMembershipReceipt` rather than an ambiguous `void`.
- Public `MembershipSnapshot` preserves state, unsigned decimal revision and
  revoke generation, correlation fields, and audit timestamps. It does not
  reduce central authority to a boolean.
- `StrictTransport` implements that public contract directly. There is no
  parallel translating adapter: the former boolean `HttpCentralApiClient` and
  its unused opt-in config were removed.
- `ApplyMembershipProjection` and `RefreshAccountMembership` require the
  caller to provide product and correlation ID. Only `MembershipState::Active`
  permits a local positive projection; every failure remains deny-by-default.
- The provider deliberately does not bind the client, projection, middleware,
  or reference composition. The runtime-registration test proves that this
  remains true with no hidden environment switch.
