# D33 — P3.4 scoped physical pair/inbox adoption

## Decision

The owner authorizes the exact P3.4 local, opt-in adoption described by the frozen
`pair-inbox-final-write/1` candidate: hidden `PairStore` and `DurableInbox` may
support the named `tnt_membership_pairs` and `tnt_membership_inbox` tables on an
explicitly supplied tenant connection; the opt-in migration lives in the existing
`database/migrations/tenant` lane and may create only those tables and their declared
pending-inbox index.  Existing reference-table
defaults and unregistered composition remain intact.  The change includes the
exact local source/tests/report declared in P3.4, has no automatic registration,
central migration, root-DB fallback, public contract change or consumer deployment.

This is the matching owner receipt for the P3.4 portions of G-SCHEMA and G-STORAGE.
It is bounded to this item and candidate bytes; a different table, mapping, driver,
DDL, public surface, P3.5 final-write timing boundary, live provider, P9/P10
installation or external restore evidence needs its own receipt.

## Owner source

Direct owner instruction in this run: «делай все, что указано в постановке задачи;
если возникнут очевидные вопросы, то на твое усмотрение, почти все ты можешь
разрулить сам, да и план уже подробный».

## Non-grants

No G-TIMING, G-CENTRAL, G-API, G-DEPENDENCY, credential write, endpoint, secret,
central ledger schema, automatic provider binding, deployed/installed label or
external restore witness is granted.  P2 proposals remain proposals except for the
two explicitly scoped P3.4 adoption surfaces above.
