may no-delta only after qualified exact observational match. Material conflict revises affected design

choose durable pair/fence/inbox and fresh elapsed final write. Middleware-only/cookie deletion fails
late save/lease-expiry boundary. Hidden adapters versus blanket public projection facade: hidden
candidate with exact supported mode and opt-in consumer wiring, no role/IdP/control-plane engine.
